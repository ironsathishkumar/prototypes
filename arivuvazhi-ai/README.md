# ArivuVazhi — clickable prototype

## Run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser; nothing to install.

## Included
- Welcome and student journey entry point
- Exam selection, goal, target date, and study availability setup
- Baseline assessment and sample learning profile
- Personalized starter plan and student home dashboard
- Topic practice with answer feedback and explanation
- Mistake book and revision queue
- Mini mock instructions, sample question, results, and performance insights
- AI Coach sample conversation with grounding/source guidance

## Limitations
Sample data only; nothing is connected or saved. Exam selection, exam rules, questions, scores, and AI responses are illustrative because the initial exam and its approved content sources have not yet been confirmed.
