Correct answer: C · 48

Good work—spot the pattern.

Each number is multiplied by 2 to get the next number.

Explanation

3 × 2 = 6, 6 × 2 = 12, and 12 × 2 = 24. Continue the same rule: 24 × 2 = 48.
